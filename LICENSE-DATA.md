# Licence of the data

The files in `data/` and `digitisation/` are values read from the figures of Canfield JR and Sprague JE, *Arch Toxicol* 2025;99:287-297
(https://doi.org/10.1007/s00204-024-03887-z), which is published under the Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 licence (CC BY 4.0).
They are redistributed here under the same licence (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). Please attribute the original article first.
The four figure images in `digitisation/src_figs/` are copies of Figs 2-5 of that article, reproduced under CC BY 4.0.

The files `data/dose_ranging_*` contain numbers read from Canfield JR, Fry KK, Sprague JE, *Drug Alcohol Depend* 2025;272:112710 (CC BY-NC-ND 4.0, https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc-nd/4.0/). Numerical values are reported here with attribution for non-commercial research use; none of that article's figures or tables are reproduced or adapted, and the figure images are not included in this repository. Please cite the original article.
